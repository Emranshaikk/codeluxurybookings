At a Glance: Renting a Yacht in Monaco

A professional Monaco yacht charter currently ranges from €3,500 to €25,000 per day for day charters, and €80,000 to over €2,500,000 per week for crewed superyacht charters. For weekly charters in Monaco and French Riviera waters, pricing is subject to a mandatory 20% VAT (Value Added Tax) and an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) of 40% of the base rate to cover operational fuel, elite provisioning, and Monte Carlo harbor dockage fees.

What it Costs to Charter a Yacht in Monaco

Monaco stands as the epicentre of global wealth and maritime prestige. When evaluating the yacht charter in Monaco cost, it is essential to look beyond the base charter rate listed on brokerage shortlists. Under MYBA contract standards, the base rate covers the hire of the vessel and the crew's salaries. All operational and regulatory expenses are accounted for additionally to preserve complete flexibility for our clients. Planning a luxury yacht rental Monaco excursion demands a clear understanding of Mediterranean tax structures.

To calculate a realistic budget for your Monaco yacht booking, you must factor in the two primary additional financial pillars:

  • Local Tax (20% VAT): Value Added Tax in the Principality of Monaco and adjacent French waters is calculated at 20% of the base charter fee. A qualified broker will route your maritime itinerary strategically to minimize tax exposure where legally permissible under local maritime laws.
  • Advance Provisioning Allowance (40% APA): Cruising the French Riviera, running high-performance yacht tenders, and docking in high-demand marinas like Monaco's Port Hercule requires significant operational expense. A 40% APA is collected upfront to fund fuel consumption, Michelin-tier gourmet catering, and premium dockage fees. Any unused APA funds are refunded to the client in full upon disembarkation.

By combining the base fee, the 20% local tax, and the 40% APA, the total all-inclusive security capital needed for a seamless charter experience is typically 160% of the initial base quote.

Monte Carlo Harbor and Marina Details

Monaco’s maritime infrastructure is divided into two main harbors, both representing the absolute peak of marine engineering. When planning your yacht rental Monte Carlo, securing the correct berth and understanding local harbor restrictions is paramount.

Port Hercule: The Global Stage

Port Hercule is Monaco’s deep-water port, capable of hosting the largest megayachts in the world. Centred in the heart of the La Condamine district, this port is the focal point of the Monaco Grand Prix. Berths here are highly sought after and require months of planning. Elite Luxury Bookings maintains relationships with local harbor masters to secure priority berthing slots wherever possible.

Port de Fontvieille: Discreet Luxury

Located to the west of the famous Monaco Rock, Port de Fontvieille offers a more sheltered, quiet environment. Surrounded by fine dining restaurants and luxury residential developments, it is the port of choice for local residents and clients seeking absolute discretion away from the public eye.

Both harbors maintain strict environmental standards and zero-discharge policies. All crewed yachts chartered through Elite Luxury Bookings are fully compliant with Monaco's environmental regulations.

Monaco Yacht Charter FAQ

How much does it cost to rent a yacht in Monaco?

A local boat rental Monaco day rate starts at €3,500 for a 40ft day boat and ranges up to €25,000+ for large motor yachts. Weekly base rates start at €80,000 for a 30-metre (100ft) explorer yacht and reach up to €2,500,000+ for 70m+ ultra megayachts. When selecting a Monaco yacht for rent, remember that weekly charters require adding 20% VAT tax and a 40% APA deposit to cover fuel and culinary provisioning (bringing total outlays to €128,000 to €4,000,000+).

Can I charter a yacht for one day in Monaco?

Yes, a Monaco yacht day charter is highly popular. Elite Luxury Bookings arranges day boat rentals and flybridge yachts for scenic coastal tours, lunch in Cap d'Antibes, or corporate entertainment. Day charters are typically subject to a 20% local tax, with fuel and catering billed separately.

Do I need a captain to rent a boat in Monaco?

Yes. To ensure safety and comply with local maritime regulations, all charters booked through Elite Luxury Bookings are fully crewed and include a licensed professional captain. Bareboat rentals are not permitted on our luxury fleet.

What's the best yacht size to charter in Monaco?

For day trips along the French Riviera, 50ft to 70ft yachts offer an excellent balance of speed and comfort. For overnight or weekly cruises, we recommend 100ft+ (30m+) superyachts to ensure spacious cabins, stabilizer comfort, and adequate storage for water toys.

What is the APA deposit, and is it refundable?

The Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) is a standard 40% deposit used by the Captain to purchase fuel, food, dockage, and beverages on your behalf. Every expense is tracked via an itemized ledger, and any unspent APA funds are returned to you in full.

How far in advance should I book my Monaco yacht rental?

For peak summer months (July and August) or major events like the Monaco Grand Prix, we recommend finalizing your Monaco yacht booking 6 to 9 months in advance. This ensures access to the best vessels and guarantees harbor slot reservations.

Can I dock my charter yacht at Monte Carlo during the Grand Prix?

Yes, but it requires early planning. Port Hercule berths during the Grand Prix are heavily restricted and subject to premium event rates. Elite Luxury Bookings coordinates GP berth applications and VIP hospitality access for our charter clients.
