Ibiza Yacht Day Charter: Prices From €900

A day on the water is the fastest way to see why Ibiza earns its reputation. Turquoise coves that no road reaches, a lunch stop at a beach club only accessible by boat, and — if you time it right — the short crossing to Formentera's Illetes beach, regularly ranked among the best beaches in Europe. A private yacht rental in Ibiza puts all of it on your own schedule, with no crowds and no fixed itinerary.

This guide breaks down real day-charter pricing by boat type, what's actually included, the fastest way to book a boat with a skipper, and answers to the questions people ask most before chartering a boat in Ibiza for the day.

How much does an Ibiza yacht day charter cost?

Full-day charters in Ibiza start from around €900 for a skippered day boat, rising to €1,200–€2,800 for a catamaran, and €2,800–€6,500+ for a luxury motor yacht in peak season. Price depends on:

  • Boat type — day boat, catamaran, motor yacht, or gulet
  • Season — July and August run 20–40% above May, June, and September rates
  • Duration — half-day (typically 4-5 hours) vs. full-day (7-8 hours)
  • Skipper & crew — included as standard on every charter we coordinate
  • Route — a straight Ibiza coastal day differs in cost from an Ibiza–Formentera crossing
  • Extras — lunch, DJ/sound system, water toys, and celebration add-ons

Rather than contacting multiple boat owners individually to compare, Elite Luxury Bookings coordinates availability across our vetted Ibiza fleet from one point of contact.

Which Ibiza Yacht Day Charter Is Right for You?

Choosing the right vessel depends entirely on the atmosphere you wish to create, your group dynamic, and how you envision your day on the Balearic Sea.

Best for Groups

Sailing catamarans and gulets offer the most deck space per euro, with wide, stable platforms ideal for 10+ guests, group lunches, and easy swimming access. A catamaran day charter is usually the best value when the group is the priority over speed.

Sunset Charter

A shorter evening charter (typically 3-4 hours from around €700) timed to catch the sun setting over the water — a popular add-on to a wedding, birthday, or simply a relaxed final evening on the island. Ibiza's west-facing coastline makes this one of the best sunset settings in the Mediterranean.

Bachelorette & Celebrations

Motor yachts and catamarans with an onboard sound system and DJ deck are the most requested setup for bachelorette parties and birthday celebrations. We can arrange a DJ, dedicated sound system, and decoration on request — flag this when you enquire so we match you with a boat that has the right onboard setup.

Wedding & Proposals

A private half-day or full-day charter works well for a wedding party crossing, a proposal, or an intimate ceremony on deck. Luxury motor yachts and larger gulets offer more privacy and space for photography than smaller boats — let us know the occasion and we'll recommend accordingly.

VIP Day Charter

For guests wanting the highest level of comfort and discretion, our superyacht day-charter tier includes a full crew, private chef options, and premium tender access to coves that larger vessels can't reach directly.

Formentera Day Trip

The single most popular use of an Ibiza day charter. Sail across the strait to the crystalline waters of Playa de Illetes and Ses Salines, dropping anchor for an unforgettable lunch stop and swim.

The Ibiza to Formentera Day Trip

The short crossing to Formentera is the most popular route for an Ibiza yacht day charter, and for good reason: Illetes beach, on Formentera's northern tip, is regularly ranked among Europe's best beaches for its shallow, Caribbean-clear water.

The crossing from Ibiza Town takes roughly 30–45 minutes by motor yacht, or a little longer under sail. Most full-day Formentera charters follow a similar pattern: depart mid-morning, anchor off Illetes or Ses Salines for swimming and lunch, and return to Ibiza by early evening.

Ibiza to Formentera day trip cost: because of the added fuel and slightly longer day, expect a modest premium over a standard Ibiza coastal charter — from around €950 for a day boat, up to the same tiers shown in the pricing table above for catamarans and motor yachts making the crossing.

Skipper, Crew & What's Included

Every charter we coordinate includes a skipper as standard — you don't need any boating license or experience. Depending on the boat category, you can also expect:

  • ✓ Standard inclusions: skipper, fuel for a typical day route, basic safety equipment, and soft drinks/water.
  • ✓ Lunch included: many of our partner boats offer lunch as part of the package, either catered onboard or arranged at one of Ibiza's beachfront restaurants with dinghy pick-up — ask us to confirm this when requesting a quote, as it varies by boat.
  • ✓ Fuel beyond the standard route: typically billed separately if you request a longer-than-usual route.
  • ✓ Add-ons available on request: DJ and sound system, water toys (paddleboards, snorkelling gear, towables), decoration for celebrations, and private chef service on larger yachts.

Where You'll Depart From

Most of our Ibiza day-charter fleet departs from Marina Botafoch, Ibiza Town's premier marina and the most convenient base for reaching both the island's western coves and the Formentera crossing.

Depending on the boat and your accommodation, we can also arrange departures from Marina Ibiza or San Antonio, particularly useful if you're staying on the island's west side and want to prioritise a sunset-facing route along Cala Conta and Es Vedrà.

Ibiza Yacht Charter Pricing by Season

Ibiza's charter season runs roughly May through October, with pricing shifting significantly by month:

Period Relative Pricing Notes
May, June, September Baseline (lowest) Warm water, fewer crowds, best value
July +20–30% Peak demand begins, book 1–2 weeks ahead
August +30–40% Highest demand of the year; book 2–4+ weeks ahead for your preferred boat and date
October Back to baseline Water still warm, fewer crowds, shoulder-season rates return

If your dates are flexible, shifting even one or two weeks outside of peak August can meaningfully lower the cost of the same boat while offering a far more tranquil cove-anchoring experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does it cost to hire a yacht for the day in Ibiza?

Full-day charters start from around €900 for a skippered day boat, rising to €2,800–€6,500+ for a luxury motor yacht in peak season. Catamarans and gulets typically fall between €1,200 and €3,500 per day. Half-day charters (4-5 hours) are available from roughly two-thirds of the full-day rate.

2. Is a skipper included in the price?

Yes. Every charter we coordinate includes a licensed skipper as standard — no boating experience or license is required from you. If you'd prefer to self-drive a small, license-free boat (under 5 metres, within 2 nautical miles of shore), we can also arrange that separately from around €450/day.

3. Do I need a boating license to rent a boat in Ibiza?

Not for a skippered charter — the skipper handles navigation. If you want to drive yourself, Spain allows license-free daytime navigation on motorboats up to 5 metres with engines up to 15hp, and sailboats up to 6 metres, within 2 nautical miles of the coast. Anything larger requires a recognised license (such as an ICC).

4. How much does an Ibiza to Formentera day trip cost?

Expect a modest premium over a standard Ibiza coastal charter due to the longer route and extra fuel — from around €950 for a day boat, following the same per-category pricing shown above for catamarans and motor yachts.

5. What's the best boat for a bachelorette party or celebration?

Motor yachts and catamarans with an onboard sound system and DJ deck are the most popular choice for celebrations. Let us know the occasion when you enquire and we'll match you with a boat that has the right setup, plus optional DJ and decoration add-ons.

6. Can I book a half-day charter instead of a full day?

Yes. Half-day charters (typically 4-5 hours) are available across most of our fleet and cost roughly two-thirds of the full-day rate — a good option for a sunset charter or a shorter trip alongside other island plans.

7. Is lunch included in the charter price?

It varies by boat. Some of our partner vessels include catered lunch onboard or a stop at a beachfront restaurant with dinghy pick-up; others charge this as an add-on. We'll confirm exactly what's included for your specific boat when you request a quote.

8. How much more expensive is August compared to other months?

August typically runs 30-40% above baseline May/June/September pricing, reflecting peak demand. July is similarly elevated (20-30% above baseline). If your dates are flexible, shifting outside of peak August can noticeably lower the cost for the same boat.

9. What's the best departure marina in Ibiza?

Marina Botafoch in Ibiza Town is the most commonly used departure point for day charters, offering convenient access to both the island's coastline and the Formentera crossing. Marina Ibiza and San Antonio are also available depending on your boat and accommodation.

10. How much does it cost to charter a yacht like on Below Deck?

The crewed superyachts featured on Bravo's Below Deck are typically weekly charters running well into six figures, in a different category from a single-day Ibiza charter. If you're drawn to that level of crewed luxury for a shorter trip, our VIP/superyacht day-charter tier (from €6,500/day, full crew) is the closest comparable experience available for a single day rather than a full week.
