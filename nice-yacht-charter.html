Maritime Heart of Nice

Vieux Port de Nice & The Panoramic Baie des Anges

Unlike purpose-built resort marinas, Vieux Port de Nice (Port Lympia) is a deeply authentic Mediterranean harbor framed by Venetian-style ocher facades, traditional wooden pointus fishing boats, and deep-water quays for modern superyachts:

Port Lympia Basin

Nestled directly beneath the towering Castle Hill (Colline du Château), Port Lympia offers sheltered boarding, VIP gangway access, and specialized fueling facilities for high-speed cruising.

Baie des Anges

The iconic crescent-shaped Bay of Angels offers wide-open cruising waters, deep blue depths, and gentle sea breezes ideal for daytime anchoring, water sports, and Seabob exploration.

Promenade des Anglais

Cruising along the Nice waterfront gives you an unobstructed panoramic view of the 7-kilometer Promenade des Anglais, the iconic dome of Le Negresco, and the Maritime Alps rising behind the city.

Provençal & Italianate Culture

Vieux Nice (Old Town) Shore Excursion

One of the greatest advantages of chartering from Nice is the immediate proximity between the yacht berths and the historic heart of Vieux Nice. Within a two-minute stroll from your yacht gangway, immerse yourself in authentic Riviera culture:

Cours Saleya Flower & Food Market

Experience the vibrant sensory morning market of Cours Saleya. Your yacht chef can personally source heirloom tomatoes, artisan socca, fresh truffles, and rare Provençal cheeses directly for your afternoon lunch at anchor.

Gastronomy & Castle Hill Lookouts

Wander through narrow cobblestone alleys lined with Baroque chapels, artisan boutiques, and Michelin-starred dining (such as JAN and Chantecler), or ascend Castle Hill for sunset panoramas over Port Lympia.

Frequently Asked Questions: Nice Yacht Charter

How much does a day charter cost in Nice?

A day charter in Nice typically costs between €2,800 and €6,000 for a 38ft to 48ft luxury day cruiser (such as a Pardo 43 or VanDutch 40), €7,500 to €18,000 for a 55ft to 75ft flybridge motor yacht, and €16,000 to €30,000+ for an 80ft+ executive yacht. Charters embarking in French waters are subject to 20% French VAT plus fuel consumption.

How much does it cost to rent a yacht in Nice for a week?

Weekly luxury yacht charter rates departing Nice start from €45,000 to €85,000 for an 80ft to 100ft motor yacht, €90,000 to €180,000 for a 100ft to 140ft bespoke superyacht, €190,000 to €450,000 for a 140ft to 180ft elite mega yacht, and €500,000 to over €1,800,000 per week for 180ft+ ultra-luxury megayachts. Weekly charters operate under standard MYBA contract terms with 20% French VAT and a 30% to 40% Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA).

How does yacht charter Nice airport transfer coordination work?

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE) is located just 7 km from Vieux Port de Nice. Elite Luxury Bookings arranges seamless 'Fly In, Sail Out' coordination: our private chauffeur meets you at the commercial VIP lounge or private aviation terminal (FBO), transfers your party and luggage to Port Lympia in under 15 minutes, and steps you directly onto your fully provisioned charter yacht for immediate departure.

Is a boat charter Nice with captain included?

Yes. Every private boat charter in Nice arranged through Elite Luxury Bookings includes a licensed, certified Master Captain. For larger motor cruisers and superyachts, a dedicated crew including stewardesses, deckhands, and an executive chef is also provided.

Can I rent a boat in Nice without a license?

Under French maritime regulations, bareboat rentals without a coastal skipper license are restricted exclusively to small, underpowered craft under 6 HP, which cannot safely navigate beyond immediate harbor confines. All luxury yachts, speedboats, and catamarans in our Nice fleet include a certified commercial captain, eliminating all licensing requirements, safety stress, and security deposit liabilities for you.

How many guests can a charter yacht in Nice accommodate?

Under French and international maritime safety regulations, private day charter yachts carry a maximum of 12 guests while cruising underway, plus captain and crew. For overnight weekly charters, luxury staterooms comfortably host between 8 and 12 sleeping guests across 4 to 6 en-suite cabins.

Can we charter a yacht from Nice to Monaco or Nice to Cannes?

Yes. A yacht charter Nice to Monaco covers approximately 10 nautical miles and takes 30 to 40 minutes, cruising past Cap de Nice and Cap d'Ail into Port Hercule. A yacht charter Nice to Cannes covers approximately 16 nautical miles across the Baie des Anges and takes 45 to 60 minutes, arriving off the Boulevard de la Croisette.

What is included in a sunset boat charter Nice?

A sunset boat charter in Nice lasts 2.5 to 3.5 hours departing Vieux Port in the golden hour. It includes a licensed captain, fuel for cruising the Baie des Anges, sommelier-selected chilled rosé or vintage champagne, gourmet Mediterranean tapas, and panoramic water-level views of the illuminated Promenade des Anglais and Castle Hill.

Where do yachts dock and embark in Nice (Port Lympia vs. Baie des Anges)?

Primary embarkation in Nice takes place at Vieux Port de Nice (historically Port Lympia), situated beneath Castle Hill with secure yacht berths, fuel docks, and immediate proximity to Old Town Nice. For guests anchored in the Baie des Anges or at luxury beachfront hotels, private tender pickups can also be coordinated.
