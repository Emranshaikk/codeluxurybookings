Regional Cruising Corridor

The French Riviera Yacht Charter Scene

Stretching along France’s southeastern Mediterranean coast from the Italian border through Monaco to the golden shores of Saint-Tropez and the Îles d'Hyères, the French Riviera boat charter corridor represents the undisputed capital of international luxury yachting. Sheltered by the Maritime Alps and blessed with more than 300 days of annual sunshine, the Côte d'Azur provides calm morning seas, crystalline turquoise anchorages, and Michelin-starred coastal dining.

Whether you seek a French Riviera private yacht charter for a high-speed day run between beach clubs or a leisurely multi-day French Riviera sailing charter exploring quiet calanques, the region offers five core embarkation harbors:

Monaco (Port Hercule)

The epicenter of ultra-wealth, deep-water megayachts, and Grand Prix trackside hospitality.

Antibes (Port Vauban)

Europe's largest pleasure port, featuring the historic Quai des Milliardaires.

Cannes (Vieux Port & Canto)

Film Festival glamour, Croisette luxury, and 15-minute access to the Îles de Lérins sanctuary.

Saint-Tropez

Legendary Pampelonne beach clubs, Club 55, and historic pastel Old Port quays.

Pure Maritime Elegance

Sailing & Sailboat Charters on the French Riviera

While powerful motor yachts dominate the headlines in Monte Carlo, a French Riviera sailing charter or French Riviera sailboat charter unlocks an entirely distinct dimension of Mediterranean seafaring. Powered by consistent coastal thermal breezes, sailing offers silent, tranquil navigation, zero engine vibration, and an eco-conscious rhythm that connects you directly to the sea.

Luxury Cruising Catamarans

Twin-hulled catamarans (such as Lagoon 50–65, Bali 4.8, and Sunreef luxury yachts) provide massive beam widths (often 8 to 11 meters wide). They deliver unrivaled stability at anchor, shallow drafts capable of anchoring close to secluded beaches in Porquerolles, and expansive forward trampolines for sunbathing.

Classic & Modern Monohull Yachts

Monohull sailing yachts (from builders like Nautor's Swan, Oyster, and Jeanneau Yachts) appeal to pure sailing purists. With sleek lines, deep keels, and state-of-the-art rigging, they offer exhilarating performance under sail while navigating coastal waters between Cannes, Saint-Tropez, and the Golfe de Fréjus.

Côte d'Azur Wind Patterns

The French Riviera benefits from gentle morning thermal breezes (10–16 knots) building throughout the early afternoon. The bay of Golfe-Juan and the sheltered lee of Cap d'Antibes provide pristine flat-water sailing conditions, while our experienced skippers actively navigate around occasional northwesterly Mistral patterns.

Why Choose a Sailing Yacht Charter French Riviera?

Beyond the romance of hoisting sails, a sailing or catamaran charter drastically reduces fuel consumption compared to displacement motor yachts. This translates to substantial savings on your final Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA), allowing you to allocate charter funds toward Michelin-starred seaside gastronomy and vintage champagne curation.

Classic 7-Day Regional Route

Sample French Riviera Yacht Charter Itinerary

This curated 7-day French Riviera yacht charter itinerary highlights the premier anchorages, heritage ports, and culinary landmarks of the Côte d'Azur:

01 DAY ONE

Nice / Villefranche-sur-Mer to Monaco (Port Hercule)

Embark in Nice or the deep natural harbor of Villefranche-sur-Mer. Enjoy a gentle morning cruise past Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, dropping anchor at Mala Bay (Cap d'Ail) for swimming. Arrive in Port Hercule, Monaco for an evening at the Casino de Monte-Carlo and dinner at Le Louis XV - Alain Ducasse.

02 DAY TWO

Monaco to Cap d'Antibes & Billionaires' Bay

Cruise west toward the Cap d'Antibes peninsula. Anchor in Baie des Milliardaires beneath historic Belle Époque estates. Enjoy afternoon tender transfers for cocktails overlooking the cliffs at the iconic Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc before berthing at Port Vauban.

03 DAY THREE

Antibes to Cannes & the Îles de Lérins Lagoon

Make the short transit into the turquoise channel between Île Sainte-Marguerite and Île Saint-Honorat. Snorkel the Jason deCaires Taylor underwater sculptures, tender ashore for a celebratory lunch at La Guérite, and anchor overnight off the Croisette with views of Le Suquet.

04 DAY FOUR

Cannes past Massif de l'Estérel to Saint-Tropez

Cruise past the striking red volcanic cliffs of the Massif de l'Estérel. Cross the Gulf of Saint-Tropez and drop anchor at Pampelonne Beach for VIP beach club dining at Club 55 or Nikki Beach. Step ashore in the evening to explore Saint-Tropez Old Town and Place des Lices.

05 DAY FIVE

Saint-Tropez to Îles d'Hyères (Porquerolles & Port-Cros)

Navigate west to the protected nature sanctuary of the Îles d'Hyères. Anchor in Plage Notre-Dame on Île de Porquerolles—frequently voted one of Europe's top beaches—surrounded by pine forests, white sand, and crystal-clear Caribbean-grade water.

06 DAY SIX

Porquerolles Coastal Sail to Théoule-sur-Mer Calanques

A leisurely coastal return passage under full sail or cruising power. Stop for secluded swimming and Seabob exploration in the calanques of Théoule-sur-Mer before enjoying a chef-prepared sunset dinner on the aft deck in the Bay of Cannes.

07 DAY SEVEN

Cannes to Nice / Disembarkation

Enjoy a relaxed champagne breakfast at anchor off Saint-Honorat. Cruise past the Promenade des Anglais for final disembarkation at Port Lympia (Nice) or private VIP helicopter transfers directly to Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE).

Frequently Asked Questions: French Riviera Yacht Charter

What is included in a French Riviera yacht charter?

Every private yacht charter booked through Elite Luxury Bookings includes a commercially licensed Captain and professional crew, vessel insurance, standard safety equipment, and tender operations. On weekly charters, fuel, local VAT (20%), food, premium beverages, and marina berthing fees are covered through the Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA), typically calculated at 30% to 40% of the base charter fee.

How much does a French Riviera yacht charter cost?

French Riviera yacht charter prices range from €2,800 to €6,500 per day for 38ft to 45ft luxury day boats, €8,000 to €25,000 per day for 60ft to 90ft motor yachts, and €45,000 to over €2,500,000 per week for luxury superyachts and megayachts. Sailing catamarans typically rent between €18,000 and €45,000 per week. All rates in French and Monegasque territorial waters are subject to 20% VAT.

Can I sail between Cannes and Monaco on a day charter?

Yes. On a fast performance motor yacht cruising at 25 knots, the distance between Cannes and Monaco is approximately 28 nautical miles and takes just over an hour. This allows for a scenic day cruise past Cap d'Antibes, Nice, and Villefranche-sur-Mer with lunch in Monaco before returning to Cannes by sunset. On a sailing yacht, this voyage is better enjoyed as an overnight or multi-day itinerary.

What is the best time of year for a French Riviera sailing charter?

The prime sailing season on the French Riviera runs from May through October. July and August deliver the warmest waters and vibrant social scene, while June and September offer ideal thermal sailing breezes (10 to 18 knots), quieter anchorages in the Lérins Islands and Porquerolles, and pleasant Mediterranean temperatures.

How does a sailing yacht charter compare to a motor yacht on the Côte d'Azur?

Sailing yachts and catamarans offer peaceful, wind-powered cruising, significantly lower fuel expenses (reducing your final APA balance), and massive social deck spaces on catamarans. Motor yachts prioritize high-speed transits between ports like Cannes and Saint-Tropez, multi-level flybridges, and extensive indoor luxury staterooms.

Can I book a day yacht charter on the French Riviera?

Yes, day yacht charters are exceptionally popular across the Côte d'Azur, particularly departing from Cannes for the Îles de Lérins lagoon, from Nice for Villefranche-sur-Mer, or from Monaco for Cap Martin. Day charters include a licensed captain and are subject to 20% French VAT.

What are the primary departure ports for a French Riviera yacht hire?

The five principal charter hubs on the French Riviera are Port Hercule (Monaco), Vieux Port de Cannes & Port Pierre Canto (Cannes), Port Vauban (Antibes - Europe's largest pleasure port), Port Lympia (Nice), and Port de Saint-Tropez. We coordinate seamless boarding at any marina or private anchor bay along the coast.
