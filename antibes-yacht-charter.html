Financial Framework

What It Costs to Charter a Yacht in Antibes

When evaluating how much does it cost to charter a yacht in Antibes, understanding the Mediterranean contractual framework ensures complete financial clarity. Charters arranged through Elite Luxury Bookings operate under verified MYBA (Worldwide Yachting Association) protocols, where base rates secure the vessel, licensed commercial captain, and certified crew.

French VAT (20%)

Charters embarking in French waters (including Port Vauban) are subject to standard French Value Added Tax (20%). For international coastal passages transiting outside territorial waters, maritime tax optimizations are applied strictly in compliance with EU regulations.

APA (Weekly) & Fuel (Day)

For day rentals, fuel consumption is tracked via engine hours or pre-calculated into an all-in package. For multi-day and weekly charters, an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) of 30% to 40% is held in escrow to fund fuel, fine wines, and custom chef provisioning.

Iconic Maritime Epicenter

Port Vauban: Europe’s Largest Marina & Superyacht Capital

When maritime enthusiasts search for a super yacht in Antibes now, they are drawn to Port Vauban. With over 1,500 berths spanning its historic basin, Port Vauban is universally celebrated as the superyacht capital of the Mediterranean.

Quai des Milliardaires

The International Yacht Club d'Antibes (IYCA) quay was custom-engineered to host the world's largest privately owned megayachts (up to 160 meters / 525 feet), featuring private security gates and specialized deep-water draft berths.

Fort Carré Vantage

Overlooking the harbor stands the 16th-century Fort Carré, providing an iconic architectural backdrop for yacht spotting and photography as superyachts maneuver through the harbor entrance.

Yacht Spotting Etiquette

While visiting superyachts in Port Vauban draw global admirers, Elite Luxury Bookings guarantees 100% discretion and NDA privacy for all charterers boarding along the private pontoon network.

Provençal Heritage from the Sea

Old Town Antibes & The Picasso Museum Waterfront

Unlike purpose-built modern resort marinas, Antibes blends high-end yachting with centuries of authentic Provençal history. Cruising along the coastline of Vieil Antibes offers an unmatched panoramic vista:

Château Grimaldi & Picasso Museum

Viewing the imposing stone bastions of Château Grimaldi from the water provides an unforgettable cultural encounter. Pablo Picasso worked here in 1946, inspired by the radiant Mediterranean light that illuminates the rocky shoreline.

Historic Seawall Ramparts

The massive stone ramparts constructed by military architect Sébastien Le Prestre de Vauban frame the harbor mouth. A slow cruise along the ramparts offers prime views of Old Town church spires and vibrant café terraces.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to charter a yacht in Antibes?

An Antibes yacht charter costs between €2,800 and €6,000 per day for a 38ft to 45ft day cruiser (such as a VanDutch 40 or Pardo 43), €8,000 to €22,000 per day for a 55ft to 85ft luxury flybridge yacht, and €45,000 to over €1,800,000 per week for crewed superyachts departing Port Vauban. French maritime charters are subject to 20% French VAT plus fuel (for day rentals) or a 30% to 40% Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA for weekly charters).

Where to rent a boat in Antibes?

The primary location to rent a luxury boat in Antibes is Port Vauban, Europe's largest pleasure marina, located right beside Old Town Antibes and the historic Fort Carré. Boarding takes place at either the main guest pontoons or along the prestigious Quai des Milliardaires (International Yacht Club d'Antibes / IYCA) for superyachts.

How to book a yacht charter in Antibes?

Booking an Antibes yacht charter involves 5 simple steps: (1) Select your preferred dates and guest count, (2) Receive a curated vessel shortlist with all-in pricing in EUR, (3) Confirm your vessel under a standard MYBA or Mediterranean day charter contract, (4) Submit your custom provisioning and route preferences, and (5) Board your yacht at Port Vauban greeted by your captain and crew.

Is a captain included with an Antibes boat charter?

Yes. All luxury yacht and boat rentals arranged through Elite Luxury Bookings in Antibes are fully crewed with a licensed, certified professional captain included. For larger motor yachts and superyachts, a dedicated crew including stewardesses, deckhands, and a private chef are also provided.

Can I rent a boat in Antibes without a license?

In France, bareboat rentals without a coastal boating license are restricted strictly to small, underpowered craft (under 6 horsepower), which cannot safely navigate open coastal currents or reach nearby destinations. For all luxury day cruisers, catamarans, and superyachts, Elite Luxury Bookings provides fully licensed commercial captains, allowing you to relax without requiring a boat license or security bond concerns.

How many guests can a yacht in Antibes carry?

Under French maritime law and European safety regulations, private day charter yachts carry a maximum of 12 guests underway plus professional crew, regardless of vessel size. For overnight weekly charters, luxury yachts accommodate between 8 and 12 guests across 4 to 6 private en-suite staterooms.

Can we charter a yacht from Antibes to Cannes or Monaco?

Yes, route charters are extremely popular. Cruising from Antibes to Cannes is approximately 6 nautical miles and takes 25 to 35 minutes across Golfe-Juan. Cruising from Antibes to Monaco is approximately 16 nautical miles and takes 50 to 70 minutes past Nice and Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, making both destinations ideal for day trips or one-way coastal transits.

Can I book a half day boat charter or sunset boat tour in Antibes?

Yes. We offer morning and afternoon half day boat charters (typically 4 hours) starting from €1,800 to €3,500, as well as private 2.5-hour sunset boat tours departing Port Vauban from €1,200 to €2,400. Sunset cruises provide breathtaking views of the illuminated Old Town ramparts and Fort Carré while enjoying chilled champagne.

Why are there so many superyachts in Antibes?

Port Vauban is historically recognized as the superyacht capital of the Mediterranean because of its deep-water basin, state-of-the-art shipyard infrastructure, and the famous Quai des Milliardaires (Billionaires' Quay). It is one of the few Mediterranean marinas engineered to dock privately owned megayachts up to 160 meters (525 feet) in length.
