At a Glance: Cannes Yacht Charter Cost & Pricing Structure

A private Cannes yacht charter price starts from €2,800 to €6,500 per day for luxury day boats (38ft–45ft), €8,000 to €22,000 per day for executive flybridge yachts (60ft–90ft), and €45,000 to over €1,800,000 per week for crewed superyachts. Under French maritime regulations, charters in French territorial waters are subject to a mandatory 20% French VAT. Weekly charters also require an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) of 30% to 40% of the base charter fee to cover operational fuel, Michelin-tier gourmet provisioning, and port dockage.

Financial Architecture

What It Costs to Charter a Yacht in Cannes

Cannes is the undisputed crown jewel of the French Riviera for day boaters and superyacht owners alike. When calculating your total Cannes yacht charter cost, understanding Mediterranean contract protocols ensures complete financial transparency. Charters arranged through Elite Luxury Bookings adhere strictly to MYBA (Worldwide Yachting Association) terms, where the base rate secures the vessel and certified professional crew.

Two regulatory and operational components are added to your base rate:

French VAT (20%)

Charters embarking in French waters (such as Cannes, Antibes, or Saint-Tropez) are subject to standard French Value Added Tax (20%). If your cruising itinerary involves international waters transit, your broker will optimize tax compliance legally.

Advance Provisioning Allowance (30%–40% APA)

For weekly charters, the APA is held in escrow to fund variable expenses: diesel fuel, fine wines, fresh culinary provisioning, communication costs, and marina berthing fees. Any unused balance is refunded to you directly on the final day.

For day rentals (such as a Cannes yacht day charter to the Lérins Islands), fuel is either pre-packaged into an all-inclusive rate or calculated based on engine hours logged during the excursion.

Maritime Safety Regulation: The 12-Guest Rule

Under international maritime law (SOLAS / Commercial Yacht Code), all private recreational charter yachts are legally certified for a maximum of 12 passengers while cruising underway, regardless of the yacht's overall size. For groups exceeding 12 guests wishing to cruise together, our concierge coordinates tandem charters (two yachts cruising alongside) or commercial passenger-class vessels.

Berthing & Infrastructure

Cannes Harbors: Vieux Port vs. Port Pierre Canto

Cannes boasts two world-renowned deep-water marinas situated on opposite ends of the iconic Boulevard de la Croisette. Knowing where your yacht is berthed ensures effortless VIP boarding and arrival coordination:

Historic Heart of Cannes

Vieux Port de Cannes

Positioned directly beneath the historic cobblestone quarters of Le Suquet and adjacent to the iconic Palais des Festivals et des Congrès. Vieux Port is the premier embarkation harbor for luxury day cruisers, motor yachts up to 65 meters, and static red-carpet charters during major congresses.

  • Max Yacht Length: Up to 65 meters (Jetée Albert Édouard)
  • Total Berths: 727 Slips
  • Location: Quai Saint-Pierre & Jetée Albert Édouard
  • Best Suited For: Day charters, Lérins excursions, Film Festival
Dedicated Superyacht Basin

Port Pierre Canto (Port Canto)

Situated on the eastern tip of the Boulevard de la Croisette, Port Canto was the first private marina in France designed exclusively for superyachts. Featuring deep water drafts, wide turning basins, and a tranquil atmosphere away from pedestrian crowds, it accommodates vessels up to 90+ meters.

  • Max Yacht Length: Up to 90+ meters
  • Total Berths: 550 Slips
  • Location: Boulevard de la Croisette (East Promenade)
  • Best Suited For: Megayachts, sailing fleets, discreet long-term berths

Seasonality & Industry Showcase

The Cannes Yachting Festival: Timing & Charter Strategy

Held annually every September, the Cannes Yachting Festival is Europe's premier in-water boat show, attracting over 700 vessels, international yacht builders, and 50,000 luxury maritime enthusiasts across Vieux Port and Port Canto.

September vs. May: Clear Distinction

The Cannes Yachting Festival in September focuses exclusively on yacht acquisitions, sea trials, and marine innovation. It is distinct from the Cannes Film Festival held in May, which centers on cinema, fashion, and red-carpet entertainment.

High-Demand Charter Window

September offers warm sea temperatures and pleasant weather. However, because hundreds of yachts participate in the festival, private day charters and static hospitality berths must be locked in 3 to 5 months in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions: Cannes Yacht Charter

How much does it cost to rent a yacht in Cannes for a day?

A day yacht rental in Cannes typically starts from €2,800 to €4,500 for a 38ft to 40ft luxury day cruiser (such as a VanDutch 40 or Pardo 38), €5,000 to €9,500 for an open performance cruiser (Wajer 55 or Fjord 44), and €10,000 to €25,000+ per day for a 70ft to 90ft luxury flybridge superyacht. In France, day charters are subject to 20% French VAT plus fuel consumption and gourmet provisioning.

What is the Cannes Yacht Charter price for a full week?

Weekly Cannes yacht charter base rates range from €45,000 to €85,000 for 80ft to 100ft motor yachts, €90,000 to €180,000 for 100ft to 140ft bespoke superyachts, €190,000 to €450,000 for 140ft to 180ft elite mega yachts, and €500,000 to over €1,800,000 per week for 180ft+ (55m+) ultra-luxury megayachts. In addition to the base charter fee, weekly charters require 20% French VAT and an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) of 30% to 40% to cover operational fuel, berthing, and onboard food and beverage.

Renting a boat in Cannes without a license: Is it permitted?

All luxury yacht charters coordinated by Elite Luxury Bookings in Cannes include a licensed, professional Master Captain and professional crew. While French maritime rules allow small boats under 6 horsepower (sans permis) to be operated by unlicensed individuals within strict 2-mile limits, renting a boat in Cannes without a license is not possible for seaworthy luxury motor yachts, day cruisers, catamarans, and superyachts, which legally require a certified commercial skipper for passenger safety and maritime insurance compliance.

How to book a yacht charter in Cannes?

Booking a Cannes yacht charter is seamless: 1) Submit your preferred charter dates, passenger count, and desired cruising route; 2) Review our curated shortlist of vetted Cannes yachts; 3) Confirm your chosen vessel under an official MYBA charter agreement with secure escrow deposit; 4) Complete a custom preference sheet for gourmet dining and beverage requests; 5) Board directly via private VIP gangway at Vieux Port or Port Canto.

Where to rent a yacht in Cannes (Vieux Port vs. Port Canto)?

Cannes has two world-class marinas located at opposite ends of the Boulevard de la Croisette. If you are wondering where to rent a yacht in Cannes: Vieux Port de Cannes (beneath Le Suquet hill, adjacent to the Palais des Festivals) is the primary base for day cruisers, tenders, and motor yachts up to 65 meters. Port Pierre Canto (at the eastern end of the Croisette) is a dedicated superyacht marina with deep drafts designed for large luxury motor yachts and sailing vessels up to 90+ meters.

Can I book a day trip by yacht from Cannes to the Lérins Islands?

Yes. A day trip by yacht from Cannes to the Lérins Islands (Île Sainte-Marguerite and Île Saint-Honorat) is the most sought-after day charter route on the French Riviera. The islands are just a 15-minute cruise from Cannes harbor. Guests can anchor in the protected turquoise lagoon, swim around the underwater eco-museum sculptures, tender to La Guérite for lunch, or explore the fortified monastery on Saint-Honorat.

How much is a yacht charter Cannes to Saint-Tropez or yacht charter Cannes to Monaco?

A yacht charter Cannes to Saint-Tropez (Pampelonne Beach) takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes on a fast motor yacht and typically ranges from €4,500 to €15,000+ depending on boat size, with additional fuel costs estimated between €800 and €2,200. A yacht charter Cannes to Monaco takes about 60 to 75 minutes, passing Cap d'Antibes and Villefranche-sur-Mer, with similar vessel rates.

Is a yacht charter Cannes with captain included?

Yes. Every private booking with Elite Luxury Bookings is a yacht charter Cannes with captain included. Larger motor yachts and superyachts also include deckhands, stewardesses, and an executive private chef dedicated to your group's hospitality and safety.

How many people can a yacht in Cannes hold?

Under international maritime law (SOLAS / Commercial Yacht Safety Code), private yacht charters are legally limited to a maximum of 12 guests while cruising underway, regardless of the vessel's length. However, for static event charters docked at Vieux Port or Port Canto (such as for the Cannes Film Festival or MIPIM), superyachts can host between 50 and 150 guests with port authority permits.

Can I book a yacht charter in Cannes for a birthday or yacht rental Cannes for a group?

Yes. A yacht charter in Cannes for a birthday or a yacht rental Cannes for a group event includes champagne receptions, onboard DJ sound setups, water toys (SeaBobs, e-foils, paddleboards), and reservations at famous waterfront venues like La Guérite on Sainte-Marguerite island.

What is included in a sunset yacht charter in Cannes?

A sunset yacht charter in Cannes typically lasts 3 to 4 hours (usually 18:00 to 21:30). It includes a dedicated captain and crew, fuel for local bay cruising, a chilled champagne welcome, and gourmet aperitif platters while anchored against the backdrop of the red Estérel cliffs and the illuminated Croisette.

How does the Cannes Yachting Festival impact yacht charter availability?

The Cannes Yachting Festival takes place every September across Vieux Port and Port Canto, showcasing over 700 boats. It is Europe's premier in-water boat show. During festival week, harbor berths and day charters are in extreme demand and should be booked 3 to 6 months in advance. Note that the Yachting Festival in September is entirely separate from the Cannes Film Festival, which occurs annually in May.
