FAQs:
Q: How much does a private boat from Mallorca to Ibiza cost?
A: A private boat charter from Mallorca to Ibiza costs from €800 per day for a standard speedboat or rigid inflatable boat (RIB) up to €25,000 or more per day for a fully crewed luxury superyacht. Motor yachts typically range between €2,800 and €12,000 per day, while luxury catamarans cost between €3,000 and €8,000 per day.

Q: How long is the private boat journey from Mallorca to Ibiza?
A: The crossing distance is approximately 150 km (93 nautical miles). The transit time ranges from 3 to 4 hours on a fast motor yacht or chase boat like a Wajer 55, about 5 to 6 hours on a luxury sailing catamaran, and up to 8 hours on a traditional wooden gulet.

Q: What is the best marina to depart from in Mallorca for Ibiza?
A: Port d'Andratx on Mallorca's southwestern coast is the premier departure point. As the closest major Mallorca marina to Ibiza, departing from here saves approximately 45 minutes of travel time compared to departing from Palma. Puerto Portals and Port Adriano are also excellent high-end alternatives.

Q: Is a private boat better than the ferry from Mallorca to Ibiza?
A: Yes. A private boat charter offers complete scheduling flexibility, absolute privacy for your group, premium catering, and dedicated crew service. Crucially, private charters allow you to anchor at exclusive spots like Formentera and Espalmador, which public ferries cannot access. For groups, the cost per person is often comparable to premium ferry tickets.

Q: When is the best time to charter a private boat from Mallorca to Ibiza?
A: The yachting season runs from May to October. June and September are considered the optimal months, offering excellent weather, calm seas, and fewer crowds. July and August represent the peak season with vibrant island energy, but require booking 3 to 4 months in advance due to high demand.

FAQs:
Q: How much does a private yacht charter from Mallorca to Formentera cost?
A: Day charters typically start from €2,500 for a luxury speedboat or motor yacht, and can exceed €10,000 depending on vessel size and season. For week-long charters, mid-range crewed catamarans cost €10,000–€18,000, while luxury superyachts start from €30,000+ per week. Rates exclude fuel, provisioning, and local marina fees.

Q: How long does the boat trip take?
A: The crossing typically takes between 2 to 3.5 hours on a modern luxury motor yacht (cruising at 20-30 knots) and 10 to 14 hours under sail for a monohull sailboat.

Q: Do I need a sailing license to charter a yacht in Mallorca?
A: For a bareboat charter in Spanish waters, you require a valid sailing qualification such as an RYA Day Skipper or ICC. Without a license, you can hire a professional skipper as part of a crewed charter package.

Q: Is it safe to sail from Mallorca to Formentera?
A: Yes, it is highly safe. However, we do not recommend making this crossing in anything smaller than 35 feet, as the Balearic Channel is subject to sudden changes in wind. A professional skipper is advised for non-sailors to eliminate risk.

Q: Can we stay overnight in Formentera?
A: Yes. You can anchor using mooring buoys at Ses Illetes or secure a rare berth in the Marina Formentera. Overnight stays should be planned and booked in advance.
